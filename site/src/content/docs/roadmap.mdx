Nothing of millpond is built yet. Each step ends with something running
on real work before the next one depends on it. A step is done when a
check passes or an observation holds. The step names are the plan's, in agentplot/blueprints'
`design/capture/plan.md`.

## millpond's own steps

<Steps>

1. **P1 · The statement.** millpond's statement is written from the
   capture design. A fresh model reconstructs millpond from it, and the
   questions it asks are ruled and folded back in.

   _Needs_ the capture design's decisions ruled. _Done when_ the
   statement lands on blueprints' `main`.

2. **P2 · The repository's rules.** This repository gains its founding
   decisions, its rules, its sluice gate and its OpenSpec configuration.

   _Needs_ P1. _Done when_ sluice protects `main` and the first change
   lands through it.

3. **P3 · The core.** `millpond land`, `run`, `tick` and
   `status --json`, with the seven steps, the raw bank, cursors and the
   write lock; the import and the built-in adapters; `millpond.toml`.

   _Needs_ P2 and flywheel-next's capture, signals and adapters crates.
   _Done when_ millpond passes the conformance pack and a capture landed
   twice writes nothing the second time. A refused line leaves the rest
   of the run landed, and a run that exits without `done` keeps its
   cursor.

4. **P4 · The tools and the Claude Code plugin.** `millpond mcp` serves
   the reader and curation tools, and the plugin ships the curate and
   read skills.

   _Needs_ P3. _Done when_ a session on a trial inbox proposes a batch
   of fifty, you change a row, and one `moves` call writes the batch as
   changed; and a reader's excerpt missing from the raw material is
   refused.

5. **P5 · The herdr plugin.** It shows the count and age of unmoved
   signals by source, and opens a proposed batch for review row by row.

   _Needs_ P3. _Done when_ its counts match `millpond status --json` on
   the trial inbox.

6. **P6 · Send an item to a worktree.** `send` makes a worktree for an
   item, writes its brief, starts an agent and records sluice's landing.

   _Needs_ P4. _Done when_ an item of two bumps sent from herdr lands
   through sluice and shows its landing, and a send from an inbox crew
   keeps is refused.

</Steps>

## Where it meets the others

- **F1 to F3, Flywheel's statement and flywheel-next.** The contract and
  the inbox enter Flywheel's statement, and flywheel-next splits out the
  capture, signals and adapters crates with the conformance pack. P3
  builds on them.
- **S1, swancloud.** The fleet's release adapter speaks the contract and
  lands into a trial inbox under `millpond run` for a week, beside its
  current path.
- **C1 and C2, crew.** crew's record says a partition's signals live in
  its inbox, and crew calls millpond: each partition's branch becomes an
  inbox, imported once, and `crew signal` writes through `millpond land`.
- **S3, swancloud.** One list names each part, its inbox, host and
  schedule. A timer per inbox runs `millpond tick`, and the doctor shows
  a row per part.
- **C3 and C4, crew.** crew's reader writes signals with millpond's
  `read`, and crew's curator works each inbox through millpond's tools.
  The planner turns accepted items into chores and units.
- **C5, crew and swancloud.** One real release travels the whole path:
  captured, curated into an item, planned, bumped by a team and landed
  by sluice.
- **F4 and F5, flywheel-next and crew.** Flywheel hosts on the same
  crates and reads an inbox millpond kept with no import, and crew
  becomes Flywheel.

headwater and eddy proceed beside millpond once its core exists, each in
its own repository: headwater's capture part lands through `millpond run`
(H6), and eddy's meeting parts pass the conformance pack's meeting checks
(E1).
