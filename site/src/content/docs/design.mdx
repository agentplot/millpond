## What each document is

- **The capture design**, `design/capture/design.md`, covers every part
  that turns an outside event into landed work: headwater, eddy,
  millpond, sluice, crew, Flywheel and the first fleet that runs them.
  `plan.md` beside it orders the work.
- **millpond's statement**, `design/millpond/requirements.md`, is
  written from that design: the inbox, the contract's intake, the seven
  steps, the reader and curation tools, moves, items and sends, the three
  setups, and what millpond never does. A fresh model reconstructs
  millpond from the statement alone, and the questions it asks are ruled
  and folded back in before the statement lands.
- **The design book** explains the design chapter by chapter, with the
  statement as its appendix.

## What binds the code

The statement binds. This repository cites its clauses by number and
never restates them. The rules a change here keeps, and the decisions
behind them, follow the statement into `docs/architecture/` and
`docs/adr/`.

Flywheel's statement owns the capture contract and the record formats,
and flywheel-next's crates implement them once. millpond builds on
those crates, so a question about the lines or the records goes to
Flywheel's statement first.
